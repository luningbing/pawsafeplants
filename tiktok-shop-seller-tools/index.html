Frequently asked questions

What should a seller subtract from GMV?Seller discounts, applicable marketplace fees, creator commission, COGS, fulfillment, expected return loss and advertising cost.
Why can an estimate differ from a payout?A payout follows a settlement window and can include refunds, shipping entries and adjustments that are not part of a simple order estimate.
