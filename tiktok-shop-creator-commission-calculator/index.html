Why 30% can work for one product and fail for another

A commission rate is affordable only after the rest of the order is paid for. The same 30% rate can be healthy on a high-margin product and loss-making on a product with heavy shipping, returns or paid media.

A practical launch rule

  1. Calculate the break-even ceiling before choosing a public commission rate.
  2. Subtract your target margin to create a safer operating ceiling.
  3. Test a small group of niche-relevant creators first.
  4. Track profit per delivered order, not GMV alone.
  5. Raise commission selectively for creators who convert profitably.

Frequently asked questions

Should a new shop offer 50% commission?Only if the order still covers fees, fulfillment, returns, ads and the intended profit. A launch rate is not free exposure.
Do creator commission and ads both count?Yes when the same order incurs both. Removing either cost can make a losing order look profitable.
Is this an accounting report?No. It is a planning estimate. Reconcile Seller Center Finance exports for actual fees and adjustments.
