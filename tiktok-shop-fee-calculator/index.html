What counts as a TikTok Shop fee?

The platform referral fee and creator commission are different charges. The referral fee follows the marketplace rate that applies to the order. Creator commission is the rate you offer or agree to for creator-driven sales.

Product cost, seller-paid fulfillment, expected return loss and ad spend are not platform fees, but they still decide whether the order makes money. That is why the calculator keeps the fee stack and the full profit estimate separate.

Formula used

net sales = selling price − seller-funded discount
fee stack = net sales × (referral fee rate + creator commission rate)
estimated profit = net sales − fee stack − product cost − shipping − expected return loss − ad cost

Frequently asked questions

How much does TikTok Shop charge sellers?There is no single rate that applies to every order. The referral fee can depend on market, category, fulfillment and current programs. Enter the rate shown for your product in Seller Center, then add creator commission separately when it applies.
Is creator commission included?It is separate from the referral fee, but both are included in the fee-stack result when creator commission is entered.
Is the result the same as my payout?No. Actual settlements can include timing differences, refunds, shipping entries and other adjustments.

Three numbers to verify

  1. The referral fee for your category
  2. The creator commission on this order
  3. Whether ads also paid for the sale
