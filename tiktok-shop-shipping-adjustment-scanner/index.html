What the scanner checks

A review queue, not another finance dashboard.

01

Late adjustments

Measures how many days passed between the original statement and the later deduction.

02

Repeated amounts

Groups identical adjustments that may have been applied across several orders at once.

03

Missing order links or reasons

Keeps missing fields visible as Unavailable instead of replacing them with zero.

04

Weight differences

Compares estimated and chargeable package weight when both values are available.

05

Evidence needed next

Suggests the settlement, label, carrier or package record that can help a human review the charge.

What this preview does not claim

  • A flagged row is not proof that TikTok must reimburse it.
  • The sample does not estimate a typical recovery rate.
  • FBT and after-sales timelines are not applied as one universal deadline.
  • The current page does not read or upload your settlement file.

Questions

Before you share any data.

Is this the finished scanner?

No. This page validates whether sellers understand and want the evidence pack. It does not currently parse a settlement file.

Will the scanner be free?

Yes. The planned base scanner and evidence-pack download are free for every user.

Will I have to subscribe to emails?

No. Entering an email unlocks the tool, but marketing updates remain a separate optional choice.

Will raw settlement files be uploaded?

The planned scanner will process raw files in browser memory. Raw files and customer PII will not be included in saved examples, analytics events or exported reports.
