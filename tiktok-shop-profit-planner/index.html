Is this a TikTok Shop profit calculator or a full accounting report?

It is a planning calculator and browser-only operating report starter. It can estimate contribution and reconcile mapped finance rows, but it is not a tax, accounting, or marketplace compliance system.

Can I import TikTok Shop Seller Center Orders and Finance exports?

Yes. Use one or more Orders CSV files and a Finance XLSX export. The mapping runs in your browser, drops PII fields before preview, and keeps raw files out of the safe aggregate report.

Why can the profit report show unavailable?

The report does not silently invent missing costs. COGS, fulfillment, ad cost, or settlement linkage can be incomplete, so the planner marks profit as unavailable or partial until the required source fields are supplied.
